    Questions

    Before you write

    How much does it cost?

    It depends on the amount, the floor, the lift and dismantling. The better the photos and description, the more accurate the quote. We confirm the final price before we start.

    What is the smallest job?

    About half a room of things. We don’t take single pieces of furniture.

    Can you come and look first?

    Yes, for free, if photos aren’t enough.

    Do I need to be there?

    No. We agree on the keys when quoting and send you photos of the empty space when we’re done.

    What happens to the things?

    We sort them and take them to PSZOK.

    How far do you travel?

    Wrocław and the area around it, up to about 70 km.

    How can I pay?

    Cash, BLIK or bank transfer. We issue invoices without VAT.

    Can you clean up afterwards?

    Yes, as an extra service. Tick it in the job card and we include it in the quote.

    What don’t you take?

    Asbestos and medical waste. Paint, chemicals and rubble only by arrangement.
